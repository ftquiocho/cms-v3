Academic Administration – Help

Academic Administration is your central hub for managing the academic structure, policies, calendar, and institutional settings.

STRUCTURE – Manage Colleges, Departments, Courses, Subjects, and Curriculum mappings.

POLICIES – Configure grading systems, grade remarks, score settings, and autolapse rules.

CALENDAR – Define semesters, schedules, and graduation dates.

INSTITUTION – Update school info, branding (logo/signature), and generate reports.

Tip

  • Use the Search box to filter modules by name.
  • Switch between Grid and List views using the toggle buttons.
  • Click any tile to navigate directly to that submodule.
  • The All tab shows every submodule in one unified view.
  • Recent activity shows the latest updates – click View or Review for details.

For additional support, contact the System Administrator.
